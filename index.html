GreenPace

GreenPace is a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers, making it easier to sell and access fresh produce at fair prices. Farmers can list their products, manage orders, and communicate with buyers, while buyers can browse, compare, and purchase with confidence. By reducing reliance on middlemen, GreenPace creates a simple, transparent, and more efficient way for agricultural trade to happen.
